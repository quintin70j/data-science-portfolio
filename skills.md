## Approach for future public studies

1. Define the business question and a measurable success criterion.
2. Document the public or synthetic data source, usage terms, and limitations.
3. Inspect missing values, distributions, outliers, and potential data leakage.
4. Establish a simple baseline before introducing more complex models.
5. Use appropriate train/test splits, cross-validation, and training-only parameter tuning.
6. Report relevant metrics, uncertainty, tradeoffs, and limits of generalization.
7. Communicate findings with clear visuals and reproducible instructions.

This is a documentation standard for upcoming portfolio work, not a claim that those studies are already complete. Private project implementations remain confidential.
